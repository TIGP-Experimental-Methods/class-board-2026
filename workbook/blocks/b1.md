# B1 — Precision inputs (your block)

**What it does.** Eight analog inputs, **±10 V, 16-bit**, on the front-panel SMAs AI1–AI8, read by one **ADS8688** ADC over SPI (500 kSPS aggregate). It is the instrument's eyes: everything the phone plots or alarms on from the outside world comes through here. The Scope tab (`stream` / `capture`, PROTOCOL.md §6) reads this ADC.

**Parts in your zone (≈ 32).** ADS8688IDBTR (TSSOP-38, `/CS` = GPIO 10) with its supply decoupling (100 nF + 10 µF on AVDD = +5VA; DVDD = +3V3), REFCAP 10 µF + 1 µF to AGND, REFIO 100 nF, 10 kΩ pull-ups on /RST and /PD, 33 Ω series on SDO; **eight identical input networks**: SMA → 1 kΩ → node with 1 nF to AGND and a BAV99 dual diode clamping to ±12 V. Channels 7 and 8 have a 3-pad solder jumper (default: SMA; alternate: the instructor's signal-conditioning chain).

**Schematic.** Full PDF page *B1* (link on the course site from Tue 09-16). Your **gapped sheet** is missing one complete input network (AI4: 1 kΩ, 1 nF, BAV99) and the ADC's AVDD 100 nF / 10 µF pair — you place them in E6 from the PDF. Your **LCSC part** (E8): the BAV99, C2500.

**Your zone on the PCB.** `ZONE_B1`, front-middle of the board, the eight networks in a row along the front edge in SMA order, the ADC in the middle on AGND copper; the AGND–GND star point sits on this zone's boundary. Rules: nothing digital under the analog inputs; SPI enters from the socket side only; nothing on layer 2; stay inside the zone.

## The two questions (E3, HW1)
1. **Why is there a 1 kΩ resistor in series with each input?** *(think: what happens when someone connects 30 V, or a static discharge; where does the current go; what does the 1 nF do together with it)*
2. **Where does your block's analog ground join the rest of the board, and why only there?** *(think: return currents of the relays and the USB power on one side, microvolt signals on the other)*

Harder pair for L2: which pins carry the fastest signal in your block? What is the input bandwidth of the 1 kΩ + 1 nF network, and is it above or below the sample rate you will use?

## Your number (E5 → E13b)
**Noise floor of one input, in LSB rms**, with the SMA shorted by a terminator: capture 4000 samples with the Scope tab or `instrument capture`, compute the standard deviation. Write the expected value in `SPEC.md` now (a good 16-bit ADC on a quiet board: a few LSB), the measured one in the wrap-up.

## Your software (E11)
Commands `read_all` → 8 volts, `set_range {ch, range}`; status `ai1…ai8`, `range`; the Scope tab's `stream` / `capture` on this ADC. SIM: return slow sines plus noise. Alarm rule: `ai1 > 9 V → relay 1 off` (over-range protection story for the demo).
