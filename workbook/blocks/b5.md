# B5 — Digital I/O & timing (your block)

**What it does.** Eight **5 V TTL outputs** (six general DIO + two fast trigger outputs) on screw terminals, and the front-panel **TRIG** SMA that can be an input or an output (direction from GPIO 39 — a GPIO is a general-purpose pin on the microcontroller). It is how the instrument talks to other lab equipment in the language they all speak — 5 V TTL — while the ESP32 stays at 3.3 V behind the buffers. A **TCXO footprint** (not populated) waits for a future 10 MHz reference.

**Parts in your zone (≈ 15).** SN74AHCT541 octal buffer at +5V (3.3 V in, TTL out; A1–8 from GPIO 41, 42, 47, 40, 8, 9, 14, 3; /OE tied low); 74HCT125 quad buffer for the two fast outputs (GPIO 18, 21 → terminals; the spare two channels tied off); **SN74LVC1T45** level translator for TRIG (A side +3V3 = GPIO 38, B side +5V → 100 Ω → SMA, DIR = GPIO 39; BAV99 clamp on the B side); 100 nF at every supply pin; 5 × 2P terminals (8 signals + 2 GND); the DNP TCXO with a 0 Ω link.

**Schematic** (the circuit drawing)**.** Full PDF page *B5*. Your **gapped sheet** is missing the 74HCT125 fast-out channel parts, one TTL terminal and the buffer decoupling. Your **LCSC part** (E8; LCSC is the parts catalogue of JLCPCB, the factory that makes our boards): the 74AHCT541, C50989.

**Your zone on the PCB** (the outlined region of the physical board that is yours to route)**.** `ZONE_B5`, right-middle: buffers near the socket end, terminals along the rear edge; the `FAST` nets (a net is a set of pins that are connected together) short, with GND adjacent.

## Your design number (E3)
**Does TRIG reach a TTL high into a 50 Ω input?** The 74LVC1T45 B side drives 5 V through 100 Ω into the SMA. Into a 1 MΩ scope input: fine. Into a 50 Ω-terminated instrument: compute the voltage at the load (include a few ohms of driver output resistance) and compare with the TTL V_IH of 2.0 V. If it fails, propose a fix and quantify it: a smaller series resistor (what current does the driver then need, against its 32 mA rating?), two buffer channels in parallel, or a dedicated 50 Ω line driver. *Expected magnitude:* ≈ 1.6 V — **fails TTL into 50 Ω**; 33 Ω gives ≈ 3 V but ≈ 60 mA.
