# B3 — Signal generation (your block)

**What it does.** Two analog outputs **AO1, AO2** on the front panel: **−10 … +10 V, 16-bit**, DC or waveforms, from one **DAC8563** and an **OPA2192** dual op-amp. It is the instrument's hands: the demo's first item ("a waveform out, seen on the scope and on the instrument's own input") is yours.

**Parts in your zone (≈ 15).** DAC8563SDGSR (VSSOP-10; AVDD = +5VA; internal 2.5 V reference out on `VREF_DAC` with 100 nF; /SYNC = GPIO 5, DIN = MOSI, SCLK); OPA2192 on ±12 V, each channel a **difference amplifier** with a 10 kΩ / 40 kΩ network: `AO = 4 × (DAC − VREF)`, so 0–5 V in gives −10…+10 V out; per output a **49.9 Ω series resistor** then the SMA, with a BAV99 clamp to ±12 V; 100 nF at every supply pin.

**Schematic.** Full PDF page *B3*. Your **gapped sheet** is missing the AO2 difference-amp resistor set and the AO2 49.9 Ω / clamp. Your **LCSC part** (E8): the OPA2192, C110074.

**Your zone on the PCB.** `ZONE_B3`, front-left: DAC on the socket side, op-amp next to the SMAs, ±12 V decoupling at the op-amp pins, the feedback resistors tight to the op-amp.
