# B2 — Power & rails (your block)

**What it does.** Turns 5 V from **USB-C or a 5 V barrel jack** into every rail the board needs: raw **+5V** (dev board, relays, logic), **+3V3** (logic, optos, OLED), and clean isolated **±12 V** for the analog blocks, from which **+5VA** feeds the ADC and DAC. If your block is wrong, nothing else works; if it is right, nobody notices. Rail LEDs and test points make it visible.

**Parts in your zone (≈ 20).** USB-C 16-pin receptacle (CC1/CC2 5.1 kΩ to GND; D+/D− to the dev board's native USB), DC005 jack; per input a **polyfuse** (1812L150) and an **SS34 Schottky** for ORing, an SMF5.0A **TVS**; **two B0512S-2WR3 isolated modules** (5 V → 12 V, 2 W) wired as +12 V / AGND / −12 V, each with a 600 Ω ferrite + 10 µF at the input and ferrite + 22 µF + 100 nF at the output; **78L05** (+12 V → +5VA, SOT-89); **AMS1117-3.3** (+5V → +3V3); four green rail LEDs; a test point per rail.

**The rail-assignment rule (Decision #22).** Anything that is not voltage-sensitive runs from raw +5V or +3V3 — LEDs, the dev board, relay coils, logic buffers. ±12 V and +5VA feed **only** the analog parts (op-amps, ADC, DAC). Budget: +12 V ≈ 70–75 mA of the module's 166 mA; raw 5 V ≈ 1.0–1.2 A peak.

**Schematic.** Full PDF page *B2*. Your **gapped sheet** is missing one polyfuse + TVS pair (the jack path) and the 78L05 output capacitors. Your **LCSC part** (E8): the DC005-T20 jack, C111567.

**Your zone on the PCB.** `ZONE_B2`, rear-left: power entry along the rear-left edge; modules ≥ 15 mm from the analog zones; the modules' input capacitors at their pins; 1.0 mm traces for +5V.
