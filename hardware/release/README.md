# hardware/release — the board models the housing work uses

| File | What | How it was made |
|---|---|---|
| `class-board.stl`, `front-panel.stl` | the two boards as light meshes (board body + connectors, LEDs, OLED socket), binary STL in KiCad's frame (y down, so y = -115..-15; top surface z = 1.6) | `kicad-cli pcb export stl --subst-models --define-var TIGP_BOARD_LIB=<hardware/lib> --component-filter "J*,D*"`, converted to binary |
| `class-board.step`, `front-panel.step` | the same boards as exact STEP geometry for a CAD program, without the small passives and chips (filter: connectors J*, power modules PS*, relays K*, switches SW*, crystals Y*, fuses F*, transformers T*, the LEDs; the panel: J* and its three LEDs) | `kicad-cli pcb export step --subst-models --define-var ... --component-filter ...` — without `--no-dnp`, because the DNP flag on this board means "hand-soldered, not fitted by the factory" (dev-board sockets, link connectors, module header), and the housing needs those bodies |

One known gap in these models (found 2026-10-16 when the housing hand-ins were checked): the ESP32-S3 dev board is absent, since the filter exports connectors and LEDs only, so allow for its body hanging about 17 mm below the main board (see `workbook/housing-brief.md`) when placing a floor.

Rear connectors corrected (4 Nov 2026). The exports replace each footprint's `.wrl` model with the `.step` file of the same name (`--subst-models`). The `.wrl` files sat on the pads, but three `.step` files kept EasyEDA's original origin, so in the earlier meshes the USB-C socket J201 was half sunk into the board, the DC jack J202 sat 2.4 mm to the side of its pins, and the screw terminals J901/J903 sat 4.95 mm too deep. The three `.step` files in `lib/class_board.3dshapes/` (`USB-C-SMD_TYPE-C-6PIN-2MD-073`, `DC-IN-TH_DC005-T20`, `CONN-TH_P5.00_KF301-5.0-2P`) were moved to the `.wrl` positions, the +5 mm model offset that made up for the terminal model on the front panel (J411/J412) was removed, and both boards were re-exported. Frame: x and y are KiCad page millimetres (y negative), z = 0 is the underside of the board, and the parts sit on z = 1.6, the component side. Rear connectors in these meshes: J201 shell z 1.70 to 4.86, opening centre z 3.28 at x 18.00, mouth at y = -15.61; J202 bore axis at x 34.95, z 7.80, front face at y = -15.65; J901/J903 bodies z 1.60 to 11.50, wire-entry centres z 4.80 (x 47.38, 52.38, 61.78, 66.78); J905 unchanged, wire-entry centres z 4.60 (x 74.90, 79.90, 84.90). The panel terminals J411/J412 rest on the panel surface as before.

No housing files live here. Each student designs their own housing from `workbook/housing-brief.md` with Claude Code, in `docs/students/<name>/housing/`; the first step is to open each of these two models on its own in OpenSCAD (`import("../../../../hardware/release/front-panel.stl");` from that folder, F5) and look at it.

Re-export after a board change: the commands above, run from `hardware/`; the model paths resolve only with the `TIGP_BOARD_LIB` path variable (SETUP step 9) pointing at a complete model library: the repository keeps the `.wrl` files and a few `.step` files only, the full `.step` set lives on the shared drive (`.gitignore`), so an export against the repository's own `lib/` loses the bodies of about 24 parts and after *Update Footprints from Library* (3D models) on the boards.
