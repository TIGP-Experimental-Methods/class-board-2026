# Bring-up and validation plan — TIGP class board 2026 rev B / v0.7 (main board + front panel)

Status: **HARDWARE VALIDATION PENDING** — nothing below has been executed; every expected value is derived from the
design (datasheets, brief, `design-decisions.md`).  Record results in `logbook/sessions.md` and update
`requirements.md` (column *Status*) as tests pass.

Equipment: bench supply 5 V / 2 A with current readout; DMM (4½ digit); oscilloscope ≥ 100 MHz with two probes and a
50 Ω feed-through terminator; function generator (±10 V, 100 kHz); 4 SMA–BNC cables; insulated 5–24 V source for the
isolated inputs; a Jinhua #40729 dev board with the class firmware; the main board **without** the dev board for T-01–T-03.

For the v0.7 NMR tests T-14…T-23 additionally: a **second bench supply, 0–24 V / 5 A with a current limit** (the +VEXT
input); a **60 dB SMA attenuator** (or a 1 k / 1 Ω divider built on an SMA pigtail) to make microvolt test signals; a
function generator that reaches 100 kHz with a 40 mV output; a 10 Ω / 20 W and a 4.7 Ω / 50 W wire-wound dummy load;
a small air-core coil (≈ 1 mH, a few ohms) for the polarizer test; a 100 MHz scope with **four** channels, or two
sessions with two; and the design coil of the workbook (400 turns AWG26, 4 cm × 10 cm) with its tuning capacitor.

Safety for the v0.7 tests: **power the board from USB first and only then switch on the +VEXT bench supply** (R-43,
D-35 — the OPA564 requires VDIG before V+).  Switch off in the reverse order.  +VEXT never above 18 V with U802
fitted; +VCOIL never above 24 V (external RC snubber) or 12 V (SMBJ20A fitted).  Keep a hand away from the polarizer
terminal while a coil is connected: 13 A and 81 mJ of stored energy.

Safety: the isolated inputs and the relay contacts are the only nodes that may carry external voltage; never exceed
30 V DC / 1 A on the relay contacts (board rating, brief 4.3) or 24 V on the isolated inputs.

Firmware channel table (D-19) required for T-06: `AI1→6, AI2→7, AI3→0, AI4→1, AI5→2, AI6→3, AI7→4, AI8→5`
(ADS8688 channel select bits = AIN index).

NMR sequence used by T-19 / T-20 (circuits doc §9.3): RX_BLANK low ≥ 20 µs before TX_EN high; TX_EN high for the pulse
(417 µs = 90°, 834 µs = 180°); TX_EN low; wait 1 ms; RX_BLANK high; start the ADC burst 1.2 ms after the pulse.
Poll I_FLAG and T_FLAG after every pulse and abort the scan if either is set (a current-limit flag means the coil or
the gain jumper is wrong, a thermal flag means the duty cycle is too high).

Order of the v0.7 tests: T-14 → T-15 → T-16 → T-17 (power stage, dummy load only) → T-18 → T-19 → T-20, then the
high-current pair T-21 / T-22.  Do not connect a real coil to the TX terminal before T-17 passes on a dummy load.

Open hardware decisions the bring-up must close: D-12 (socket row spacing 25.4 mm assumed), D-25 (panel header
height), R-33 (OLED socket part availability), and for v0.7: the land patterns of every new package (F-12), the CPL
rotations in the JLC preview (F-13), the missing +VEXT / sequencing silkscreen notes (F-16), and whether the tuned
tank really reaches Q = 10 with the student-wound coil (R-35).
